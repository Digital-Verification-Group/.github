# Business Information Consistency Checklist

A simple public checklist for small businesses:
- Confirm the business name is presented consistently.
- Verify the primary phone number.
- Check the street address and service area.
- Review current business hours.
- Confirm the primary website URL.
- Review major maps, directories, and social profiles.
- Recheck details after moves, phone changes, or schedule changes.
Accurate information reduces customer confusion and helps businesses maintain a consistent online presence.
